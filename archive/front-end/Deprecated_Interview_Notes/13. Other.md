# Interview Preparation Notes / 面试补充笔记

> Use these prompts to prepare evidence-backed answers in your own words. They are not scripts to repeat. 用这些提示准备有事实支撑的回答，再用自己的表达说明；不要照背模板。
>
> Related current notes: [Interview Preparation / 面试准备](../../../engineering-foundations/career/interview-preparation.md).

## Strengths and Areas to Improve / 优势与改进点

- Choose strengths that matter for the role and support each with a concise example and result.
- Describe an improvement area in a real work context: what was difficult, what you changed, and what evidence shows progress. Do not disguise a core requirement as a harmless weakness.
- Keep claims specific enough that a follow-up question can be answered honestly.

## Overtime and Urgent Work / 加班与紧急任务

Explain how you handle an occasional urgent deadline, how you communicate capacity and trade-offs, and how you protect sustainable work. A useful answer distinguishes a genuine exception from a permanent expectation; ask for the team's actual on-call or overtime practices when that affects your decision.

## Compensation / 薪酬

State a considered range only after checking the role, level, location, and total compensation structure. Explain the assumptions behind the range and remain open to discussing scope. If you do not yet have enough information, ask about the role level and compensation components before naming a number.

## Fit for the Role / 岗位匹配

Connect three things: a requirement, evidence from work or study, and the contribution you could make in this role. Separate demonstrated experience from areas you would need to learn.

## Understanding the Organization / 对公司的了解

Research the product, customers, role, and public company information before the interview. Be clear about what you know and what you are inferring; do not present guesses as inside knowledge.

## Limited Direct Experience / 缺少直接经验

Acknowledge the gap, then identify transferable skills, a relevant example, and a concrete learning plan. Avoid claiming that enthusiasm alone replaces experience.

## Questions to Ask / 可询问的问题

Choose questions that resolve uncertainty about the work and help both sides assess fit:

- What outcomes would define success in the first few months?
- Which skills or domain knowledge are most important for this role?
- What are the team's current constraints or hardest problems?
- How does the team plan, review work, and handle urgent requests?
- Is there anything in my answers that would help clarify my fit for the role?

A brief thank-you is enough to close the interview; use the remaining time for questions that matter to your decision.
