# How should you discuss urgent work and overtime? / 如何讨论紧急任务与加班？

Explain how you handle an occasional urgent deadline, communicate capacity and trade-offs, and protect sustainable work. Distinguish a genuine exception from a standing expectation, and ask about on-call or overtime practices when they affect your decision. / 说明自己如何处理偶发紧急期限、沟通容量与取舍，并维持可持续工作。区分临时例外与长期常态；若值班或加班安排会影响你的选择，就询问团队实践。

Prepare a truthful answer in your own words. Do not promise unlimited availability or claim experience you cannot support. For role fit, compensation, strengths, and interview-question prompts, see [Interview Tips](../../../engineering-foundations/career/interview-preparation.md).
