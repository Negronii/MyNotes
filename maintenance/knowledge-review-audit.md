# Full knowledge review / 全库知识审查

This audit records accepted content only. The fixed original scope contains 116 existing substantive notes—53 active and 63 archived knowledge Markdown notes—plus exactly three new Engineering Foundations notes. Nine previously reviewed active bodies are protected and excluded from that original count. The three accepted additions bring the current active inventory to 65: 53 in-scope active originals, nine protected notes, and three new notes. Raw source and prompt packs, scripts, and attachments are outside the prose review scope and remain unchanged. The original-note review is not complete.
